## 📜 Pilot Program Scope & Terms

1. **Zero License Fee**: 60-day full platform access with zero software licensing costs for selected pilot companies.
2. **Data Confidentiality & Privacy**: Datasets uploaded during the pilot remain 100% owned by the customer and are never used to train public LLM models.
3. **Deliverables**: MODLIQER provides 1 customized Quality Passport, 1 optimal parameter safe-window report, and 1 dedicated engineering walkthrough session.
4. **Customer Commitment**: Pilot company agrees to provide qualitative feedback and participate in an anonymized case study upon successful yield improvement.
